// about

Research-driven, deployment-minded.

I'm an AI engineer focused on the part of machine learning where the model meets the real world. My work spans computer vision, deep learning, and NLP — with a particular love for video: recognizing what people are doing, tracking them across frames, and doing it fast enough to matter.

Currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence Engineering at Universität Passau, I pair academic depth in generative AI, responsible ML, and optimization with hands-on experience deploying YOLO, DeepSORT, DenseNet–Transformer, and SAM/ViT pipelines — including on resource-constrained edge devices like the Nvidia Jetson Nano.

Along the way I've published three papers at IEEE ICAECT and interned as a deep-learning engineer, turning proofs-of-concept into evaluated, measurable systems.
